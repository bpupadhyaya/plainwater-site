Visual & Sensory Assessment

Read before you test

What your eyes, nose, and tongue can tell you about water — and, just as important, what they can't. These are reference signals, never a test result: many dangerous contaminants change nothing about how water looks, smells, or tastes.

Test Kit & Meter Interpretation

Make sense of the reading

Turn a strip, meter, or kit color into a decision — what the reading means, not just what it says. These interpret a device you already have; none of this is a substitute for certified laboratory testing when a result matters.

9 features · 0 free

Purification Methods

Every way to make it safe

Every way to make water safe to drink, matched to what's actually in it — from a rolling boil to a DIY sand filter. Boiling and chemical disinfection kill or inactivate germs; filtration physically removes them; nothing here removes every hazard by itself, so when in doubt, combine methods.
