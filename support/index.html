Support

PlainWater · EqualInformation, LLC

Water-safety expertise, normally locked behind labs and public-health departments, directly in your pocket — offline, since the people who need it most often have no signal and no money for a lab test.

PlainWater is an offline-first water-safety app for iOS and Android, built and maintained by EqualInformation, LLC. It helps you find, test, and make water safe to drink, and recognize and treat basic waterborne illness — purification methods and dosing calculators, illness triage and rehydration guidance, emergency sourcing and storage, region-specific risk reference, and disaster-specific checklists, organized across 8 modules and 82 features.

PlainWater is live now on Google Play. The App Store version has been submitted and is currently in review. You can also browse the real, cited content on this site.
