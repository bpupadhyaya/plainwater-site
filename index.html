Why PlainWater exists

Every human needs water daily, with no exceptions

Safe water access isn't guaranteed across much of the world, and even in developed countries boil-water advisories and disasters are routine.

💧

Offline first, on principle

PlainWater's core guidance works with zero signal, because that's exactly when infrastructure — and connectivity — has already failed.

🔒

No accounts, no servers

No sign-up, no tracking, no ads. Sync (where offered) is serverless and platform-native — never a company-run backend.

📜

Cited, not guessed

Every guidance claim traces to a free, public, authoritative source — WHO, CDC, EPA, USGS, Ready.gov/FEMA, CAWST. Tap any citation to read it yourself.
