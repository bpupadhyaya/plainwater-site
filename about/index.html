About PlainWater

Offline water-safety guidance, by EqualInformation, LLC

Water-safety expertise — normally locked behind labs, NGOs, and public-health departments — directly in your pocket, offline, since the roughly 2 billion people who need it most often have no signal and no money for a lab test.

What it does

PlainWater helps you find, test, and make water safe to drink, and recognize and treat basic waterborne illness: purification methods and dosing calculators, illness triage and rehydration guidance, emergency sourcing and storage, region-specific risk reference, and disaster-specific checklists — organized across 8 modules and 82 features.

Why offline is the whole design, not a feature

Every human needs water daily, with no exceptions. Safe water access isn't guaranteed across much of the world, and even in developed countries boil-water advisories and disasters are routine. PlainWater matters most exactly when infrastructure and connectivity have already failed — so its core guidance works with zero signal, on principle, not as a fallback.

Cited, not guessed

Every guidance claim traces to a free, public, authoritative source — WHO, CDC, EPA, USGS, Ready.gov/FEMA, the American Red Cross, UNICEF/WHO's Joint Monitoring Programme, and CAWST. No paywalled journals, no commercial or vendor sources, even when accurate — the source itself has to be something anyone can open and verify for free. You can browse the free-tier content and its citations right now.

Privacy as a founding principle

PlainWater is local-first by design: no accounts, no cloud copies of your data, no advertising, no tracking. Core guidance stays on your device. Read more in our privacy policy.

Who we are

PlainWater is made by EqualInformation, LLC, a software company creating intelligent applications for a better life. The portfolio also includes PlainRescue (acute emergency first aid) and PlainLife (generational human health).

Where to get it

PlainWater is live now on Google Play. The App Store version has been submitted and is currently in review. This site's module browser already reflects the real, cited content shipping with the app.

PlainWater provides general educational information only — it is not a medical device, does not provide medical advice, and no feature certifies that any water sample is safe to drink. See the full disclaimer.
